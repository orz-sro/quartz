
>[!example] #### 一阶线性齐次

$$
\begin{gather}
\Large y^\prime+P(x)y=0\\ \\
\downarrow \\ 

\frac{dy}{y \cdot dx}=-P(x)\\  \\

\ln y= \int-P(x)dx +C \\ \\

\Large  y= \color{yellow}Ce^{\int-P(x)dx} \\
\end{gather}
$$



>[!hint] #### 一阶线性非齐次

$$
\begin{gather}
\Large y^\prime+P(x)y=Q(x)\\ \\
\downarrow \\ \\
容易看到\,\, 左侧可以构造一个积分 \\ \\  
\Large \downarrow \\ \\


{\large \color{yellow}拄拐上楼}  \,\,乘上 u(x)\\ \\ 


\large (1)\,\,  y^\prime\cdot u(x) +{\color{plum} P(x)\,u(x)}\cdot y= Q(x)\cdot u(x)   \\  \\
我们希望\quad {\large  {\color{salmon} u^\prime(x)=}\,\, {\color{plum} P(x)\,u(x)} }\,， 这样可以构造分部积分\\ \\ 
\Large \downarrow \\ \\
 
 \,\,\, \Large {\color{salmon} u(x)} =  {\color{cyan} e^{\int P(x)dx}}\\ \\
   \\
  \large 回带 \,\,(1)\,\,式可得 \\ \\
  
  \Large\left( y \cdot e^{\int P(x)dx} \right)^\prime = Q(x)\cdot e ^{\int P(x)dx}



 \\ \\

\Large  y=  e^{\color{orange}\int-P(x)dx}\,\,( \int Q(x)\cdot e^{\color{orange}\int P(x)dx} dx \,+\,C\,) \\
\end{gather}

$$
