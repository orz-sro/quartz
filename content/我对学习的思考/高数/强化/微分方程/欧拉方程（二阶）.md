
$$\large x^ny^{(n)}+a_{1}x^{n-1}y^{(n-1)}+\cdot \cdot \cdot+a_{n-1}xy^\prime+a_{n}y=f(x)$$

形如上式的方程称为 <mark class="hltr-blue">欧拉方程</mark> 

>[!tip] 二阶非齐次方程
 $$\large x^2y^{\prime\prime}(x)+a_{1}xy^\prime(x)+a_{2}y(x)=f(x)$$
>
求通解
>
若 $x > 0$，令 $\large x=e^t$，则
>$$
>\begin{gather}
>y = y(x) = y(e^t) = y(t) = y\\ \\
y' = \frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{dt} \frac{dt}{dx} = \frac{dy}{dt} \frac{1}{\frac{dx}{dt}} = \frac{dy}{dt} \frac{1}{e^t}\\ \\
y'' = \frac{d^2 y}{dx^2} = \frac{d}{dx} \left( \frac{dy}{dx} \right) = \frac{d}{dx} \left( \frac{dy}{dt} \frac{1}{e^t} \right) = \frac{d}{dt} \left( \frac{dy}{dt} \frac{1}{e^t} \right) \frac{dt}{dx}  =\frac{d^2y}{dt^2} \frac{1}{e^{2t}}\\ \\ 
>\end{gather}
>$$

则有  $I=y$
