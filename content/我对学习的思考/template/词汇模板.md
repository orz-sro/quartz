_Context:_    goose bumps do not make us seem larger or more [intimidating]

_Root:_         `in-` (使) + `timidate` (害怕)。



- **同义词：** `Intimidating` = `frightening`, `daunting`.
	
- **反义词：**`Intimidating` $\neq$ `使喜欢`, `不知道写啥了`.
	
- **固定搭配：** `intimidating task`, `intimidating presence`.
