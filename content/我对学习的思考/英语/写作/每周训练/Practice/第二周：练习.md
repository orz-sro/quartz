## 补充论据：There is plenty of evidence...


**句型模板**

```text
There is plenty of evidence showing/illustrating/demonstrating that + 完整句子
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 有大量证据表明，过度使用社交媒体会导致青少年焦虑。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

overuse of social media, anxiety, teenagers, plenty of evidence

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
There is plenty of evidence demonstrating that the overuse of social meida makes teenager more anxious.
```

**正确写法**

```text
There is plenty of evidence showing that the overuse of social media leads to anxiety among teenagers.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `meida` 拼写错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`media`</font>
> - ❌ `makes teenager more anxious` → <font color="#7ee787">`leads to anxiety among teenagers`</font>（`teenager` 可数名词单数不能裸用，要么用 `a` 或 `the`限定住，一般加复数，表示一部分人；"导致焦虑"用 `lead to anxiety` 更书面）
> - ⚠️ `demonstrating` 语法没错，但模板更常用 <font color="#7ee787">`showing`</font>

> [!hint] 为什么
> `lead to + 名词` 表"导致"，比 `make sb + 形容词` 更正式；`among teenagers` 点明群体。

---
### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：AI is becoming more and more advanced.
>
> 改写：用 `There is plenty of evidence showing that...` 结构。

**你的答案**

```text
There is plenty of evidence convincing that AI is becoming more and more advanced including ………….
```

**正确写法**

```text
There is plenty of evidence illustrating that AI is becoming more and more advanced.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `convincing` 搭配错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`showing / illustrating / demonstrating`</font>（`convince` 是"使某人信服"，搭配 `convince sb that`，不能直接 `convincing that`）
> - ❌ `including …………` 多余 → 句子已完整，<font color="#7ee787">删掉 `including`</font> 或补全具体内容

> [!hint] 为什么
> <font color="#79c0ff">`convince`</font>（动词）= 使信服，必须带对象：`convince sb of sth / that...`。模板里 `evidence` 后接 `showing/illustrating that` 表"表明"，主语是"证据"自己在说明，不需要"说服谁"。

## 提出论点：...but the real prize is...


**句型模板**

```text
Sth is certainly useful, but the real prize is that + 完整句子
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 阅读当然有用，但真正的收获是它能改变我们的思维方式。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

reading, certainly useful, the real prize, change the way we think

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
Reading book is certainly useul, but the real prize is that it could change our mind of thinking(感觉有点奇怪的搭配).  
```

**正确写法**

```text
Reading is certainly useful, but the real prize is that it can change the way we think.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `Reading book` → <font color="#7ee787">`Reading`</font>（泛指"阅读"不加 book；要加就用复数 `Reading books`）
> - ❌ `useul` 拼写错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`useful`</font>
> - ❌ `change our mind of thinking` → <font color="#7ee787">`change the way we think`</font>（你的直觉对了，这搭配确实奇怪）
> - ⚠️ `could` → <font color="#7ee787">`can`</font>（陈述客观事实用 can）

> [!hint] 关于"感觉有点奇怪的搭配"
> 你的语感是对的。`mind of thinking` 是中式直译（"思维的大脑"），英文不这么说。"思维方式"的地道表达是 <font color="#7ee787">`the way we think`</font> 或 <font color="#7ee787">`our mindset`</font>。`change one's mind` 是固定短语，但意思是"改变主意/想法"，不是"改变思维方式"。

---
### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：Computers are useful in many aspects. AI is becoming popular.
>
> 改写：用 `...is certainly useful, but the real prize is that...` 连接两句。

**你的答案**

```text
Computers are certainly useful in many aspects, but the real prize is that AI is becoming popular.
```

**正确写法**

```text
Computers are certainly useful in many aspects, but the real prize is that AI is becoming more and more popular.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ⚠️ `becoming popular` → <font color="#7ee787">`becoming more and more popular`</font>（原句是 "more and more advanced"，改写要保留"越来越……"的递进感）
> - ✅ 句型结构 `...is certainly useful, but the real prize is that...` 套用正确

> [!hint] 为什么
> `the real prize is that...` 后面接的是"真正的亮点/收获"，用 `more and more` 强调趋势递进，比单纯 `becoming popular` 更有力度。

## 背景句：...are bound to arise


**句型模板**

```text
With so many ..., many problems are bound to arise.
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 由于如此多的家长对孩子的学习压力一无所知，许多身心健康问题必然会产生。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

parents, pressure in study, physical and mental problems, bound to arise

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
With so many parents are ignorant of their childs' learing presure, many physical and mental health problems are bound to arise.
```

**正确写法**

```text
With so many parents knowing nothing about their child's pressure in study, many physical and mental problems are bound to arise.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `With so many parents are ignorant` → <font color="#7ee787">`With so many parents knowing nothing about`</font>（`with` 复合结构 = `with + 名词 + 非谓语`，不能再加谓语 `are`）
> - ❌ `childs'` → <font color="#7ee787">`child's`</font>（`child` 复数是 `children`，没有 `childs`；这里用单数 `child's` 或 `children's`）
> - ❌ `learing` 拼写错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`learning`</font>
> - ❌ `presure` 拼写错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`pressure`</font>
> - ⚠️ `physical and mental health problems` → <font color="#7ee787">`physical and mental problems`</font>（`health` 可省）

> [!hint] 为什么
> <font color="#79c0ff">`with` 复合结构</font>（独立主格）：`with + 名词 + doing/done/形容词/介词短语`，名词后的成分是<font color="#79c0ff">非谓语</font>，绝不能出现 `are/is` 这类谓语动词。这是你本周最高频的错误，务必记住。

---
### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：Many people move to cities. Traffic problems happen.
>
> 改写：用 `With so many..., ... are bound to arise` 结构。

**你的答案**

```text
With so many people move to cityis, many traffic problems are bound to be arise.
```

**正确写法**

```text
With so many people moving to cities, many traffic problems are bound to arise.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `people move` → <font color="#7ee787">`people moving`</font>（`with` 复合结构用非谓语 `moving`，不能用谓语 `move`）
> - ❌ `cityis` 拼写错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`cities`</font>
> - ❌ `are bound to be arise` → <font color="#7ee787">`are bound to arise`</font>（`bound to + 动词原形`，不加 `be`；`arise` 是不及物动词，没有被动）

> [!hint] 为什么
> 又是 <font color="#79c0ff">`with` 复合结构</font>：`with + people + moving`（主动用 doing）。`be bound to do` 是固定搭配"必然……"，`to` 后直接接动词原形。`arise`（出现/产生）是不及物动词，<font color="#ff7b72">没有被动语态</font>。

## 总起句：For anyone who may doubt...


**句型模板**

```text
For anyone who may doubt ..., ... provides strong evidence that ...
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 对于任何可能怀疑运动好处的人，这项研究提供了强有力的证据，证明运动能改善健康。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

exercise, benefits, provide strong evidence, anyone who doubt, improve health

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
For anyone who may doubt the good that sports will do, this search provides strong evidence that sports could improve health level. 
```

**正确写法**

```text
For anyone who may doubt the benefits of exercise, this research provides strong evidence that it improves health.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `the good that sports will do` → <font color="#7ee787">`the benefits of exercise`</font>（"好处"用 `benefits`；"运动"正式表达用 `exercise`）
> - ❌ `search` → <font color="#7ee787">`research`</font>（`search` 是"搜索/搜寻"，`research` 才是"研究"）
> - ❌ `sports could improve health level` → <font color="#7ee787">`it improves health`</font>（`it` 指代 exercise；`health level` 冗余，直接 `health`）
> - ⚠️ `sports` → <font color="#7ee787">`exercise`</font>（泛指体育锻炼用 `exercise`，`sports` 偏"体育项目/竞技"）

> [!hint] 为什么
> <font color="#79c0ff">`search`</font>（动词/名词）= 搜索、寻找；<font color="#79c0ff">`research`</font>（名词/动词）= 学术研究。一字之差意思完全不同，这是高频易混词。`benefits of exercise` 是"运动的好处"的标准搭配。

---
### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：Some people doubt advanced technologies. They changed many people's lives.
>
> 改写：用 `For anyone who may doubt..., ... provides strong evidence that...` 结构。

**你的答案**

```text
For some people who doubt the advanced technologies, the fact they changed many prople's lives provides strong evidence that 
```

**正确写法**

```text
For anyone who may doubt the ability of advanced technologies, this provides strong evidence showing that they changed many people's lives.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `For some people who doubt` → <font color="#7ee787">`For anyone who may doubt`</font>（模板固定用 `anyone who may doubt`，语气更正式）
> - ❌ 主语用错：模板主语应是"研究/事实"（`this/research`），你把 `the fact` 当主语，结构乱了
> - ❌ `the fact they changed` → <font color="#7ee787">`the fact that they changed`</font>（同位语从句缺 `that`）
> - ❌ `prople's` 拼写错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`people's`</font>
> - ❌ 句子未完成，`that` 后面缺内容

> [!hint] 为什么
> 模板结构是 <font color="#79c0ff">`For anyone who may doubt X, Y provides strong evidence that Z`</font>：`For...` 是状语，主句主语 `Y`（这项研究/这个事实）才是"提供证据"的主体。你把"事实"塞进了主语位置，导致 `provides` 的主语和 `that` 从句打架。

## 背景+观点：Among the facts is that...


**句型模板**

```text
Among the facts that have emerged about ... is that + 完整句子
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 在关于信息技术的事实中，这些先进技术正在改变人们的生活是其中之一。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

information technologies, advanced technologies, great changes, people's lives

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
Among the facts that have emerged about information technologies is that advanced technology are changing peoples' lives.
```

**正确写法**

```text
Among the facts that have emerged about information technologies is that these advanced technologies are making great changes to people's lives.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `advanced technology are` → <font color="#7ee787">`these advanced technologies are`</font>（主谓一致：`technology` 单数配 `is`，`technologies` 复数才配 `are`）
> - ❌ `peoples'` → <font color="#7ee787">`people's`</font>（`people` 本身就是复数，所有格是 `people's`，没有 `peoples'`）
> - ⚠️ `changing people's lives` → <font color="#7ee787">`making great changes to people's lives`</font>（题目强调"改变"，用 `make great changes` 更贴合）

> [!hint] 为什么
> <font color="#79c0ff">`people`</font> 是集合名词，本身表复数（人们），所有格直接加 `'s` → `people's`。`peoples` 只在表"多个民族"时才用，这里不需要。主谓一致是考研写作扣分重灾区，写完务必检查主语单复数和谓语是否匹配。

---
### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：I love you. This is a fact about me.
>
> 改写：用 `Among many facts about ... is that ...` 倒装结构。

**你的答案**

```text
Among many facts that have emerged about me is that I love you.
```

**正确写法**

```text
Among many facts about me is that I love you.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ✅ 倒装结构 `Among many facts about... is that...` 套用正确
> - ⚠️ `that have emerged` 可省略 → 原句简单，不需要"已经浮现的"这层修饰，<font color="#7ee787">`Among many facts about me`</font> 更简洁

> [!hint] 为什么
> 这是 <font color="#79c0ff">完全倒装</font>：表语 `Among many facts about me` 提前，主语是后面的 `that I love you` 从句，谓语 `is` 放在主语前。原句没有"逐渐显现"的含义，加 `that have emerged` 反而累赘。

## 过渡句：...in its early stages...


**句型模板**

```text
A is in its early stages, but is already full of B.
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 寻找合适工作的竞赛才刚刚开始，但对年轻人来说已经充满了障碍。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

find an appropriate job, early stages, full of obstacles, the young

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
The race of finding proper occupying(写job是不是更地道一点) is in its early stages, but is already full of obstacle for young man.
```

**正确写法**

```text
The race to find an appropriate job is in its early stages, but is already full of obstacles for the young.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `The race of finding` → <font color="#7ee787">`The race to find`</font>（`race` 后接 `to do` 表目的，不接 `of doing`）
> - ❌ `proper occupying` → <font color="#7ee787">`an appropriate job`</font>（`occupying` 是动名词"占用/占领"，不是"职业"；职业 = `job` / `occupation`）
> - ❌ `obstacle` → <font color="#7ee787">`obstacles`</font>（"充满障碍"用复数）
> - ❌ `young man` → <font color="#7ee787">`the young`</font>（泛指"年轻人"用 `the young`；`young man` 是"一个年轻男子"）

> [!hint] 关于"写 job 是不是更地道"
> 你的判断完全正确，就该写 <font color="#7ee787">`job`</font>。`occupy`（动词）= 占用、占领（空间/职位），它的动名词 `occupying` 不是"找工作"。"职业"的名词是 <font color="#79c0ff">`occupation`</font>（正式）或 <font color="#79c0ff">`job`</font>（通用）。另外 `the + 形容词` 表一类人：`the young`（年轻人）、`the old`（老人）、`the rich`（富人）。

---
### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：Self-driving cars are new. There are many challenges.
>
> 改写：用 `The race to... is in its early stages, but is already full of...` 结构。

**你的答案**

```text
The race in self-driving cars is in its early stages, but is already full of challenges.
```

**正确写法**

```text
The race to dominate the field of self-driving cars is in its early stages, but is already full of intrigue.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `The race in self-driving cars` → <font color="#7ee787">`The race to dominate the field of self-driving cars`</font>（`race` 后接 `to do` 表"做某事的竞赛"；"在自动驾驶领域"要补出 `the field of`）
> - ⚠️ `challenges` 可以，但原答案用 <font color="#7ee787">`intrigue`</font>（复杂局面/暗斗）更生动
> - ✅ 后半句 `is in its early stages, but is already full of...` 结构正确

> [!hint] 为什么
> <font color="#79c0ff">`the race to do sth`</font> 是固定搭配，表"争相做某事"。光写 `race in self-driving cars` 意思含糊（"在自动驾驶车里的比赛"？），补出 `to dominate the field of`（争夺……领域的主导权）才完整。

## 观点句：The very fact comes at...


**句型模板**

```text
The very fact of/that A comes at a bad/good time when ...
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 学生能从AI中获益巨大这件事来得正是时候，因为当今的先进技术为他们获取各种知识提供了便利。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

students, take great advantages from AI, good time, advanced technologies, convenient access, acquire knowledge

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
The very fact of students gaining lots of profits from AI comes at a good time when the advanced technique afford convenience for getting all kinds of knowledge.
```

**正确写法**

```text
The very fact that students would take great advantages from AI comes at a good time when the advanced technologies nowadays could provide convenient access for them to acquire all sorts of knowledge.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `The very fact of students gaining` → <font color="#7ee787">`The very fact that students would take`</font>（模板用 `The very fact that + 完整句子`，不用 `of doing`）
> - ❌ `lots of profits` → <font color="#7ee787">`great advantages`</font>（`profit` 是"利润/盈利"，"获益"用 `advantages` / `benefits`）
> - ❌ `technique` → <font color="#7ee787">`technologies`</font>（`technique` 是"技巧/手法"，`technology` 才是"科技/技术"）
> - ❌ `afford convenience for getting` → <font color="#7ee787">`provide convenient access for them to acquire`</font>（`afford` 多指"买得起"，"提供便利"用 `provide convenient access`）
> - ⚠️ 主谓一致：`technique afford` → `technologies provide`

> [!hint] 为什么
> <font color="#79c0ff">`technique`</font>（技巧、技法，如绘画技巧）vs <font color="#79c0ff">`technology`</font>（科学技术）——这是考研高频易混词。`profit`（利润，商业语境）vs `benefit/advantage`（好处、益处）。`The very fact that + 句子` 中 `that` 引导同位语从句，解释 fact 的具体内容。

---
### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：The lawsuit is bad for Uber. Uber has culture problems.
>
> 改写：用 `The very fact of... comes at a bad time for...` 结构。

**你的答案**

```text
The very fact of the lawsuti comes at a bad time for Uber has culture problems. 
```

**正确写法**

```text
The very fact of the lawsuit comes at a bad time for Uber, which is under fire for having a rough-and-tumble culture.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `lawsuti` 拼写错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`lawsuit`</font>
> - ❌ `for Uber has culture problems` → <font color="#7ee787">`for Uber, which is under fire for having a rough-and-tumble culture`</font>（两个完整句子不能用空格直接拼接，要用非限制性定语从句 `which...` 连接）
> - ⚠️ `culture problems` → <font color="#7ee787">`a rough-and-tumble culture`</font>（"企业文化问题"更地道的说法）

> [!hint] 为什么
> 这是典型的 <font color="#ff7b72">run-on sentence（流水句）</font> 错误：`...comes at a bad time for Uber` 和 `Uber has culture problems` 是两个独立句子，中间没有连词。英文里补充说明 Uber 的状况，要用 <font color="#79c0ff">非限制性定语从句</font> `, which is...`，逗号 + which 引导。

## 背景+总起：...bright spots...


**句型模板**

```text
A and B have contributed to the problems. But there are some bright spots ...
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 电子产品的广泛使用导致了读书人数的骤降，但也有一些亮点表明改善是可能的。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

electronic products, plummeting number, reading books, bright spots, improvements

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
The widespread use of electronic device have contributed to the a slump of the reading number. But there are some bright spots that it's possible to improve this situation.
```

**正确写法**

```text
The wide range use of electronic products has contributed to the plummeting number of people reading books. But there are some bright spots that suggest that improvements are possible.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `electronic device` → <font color="#7ee787">`electronic products`</font>（泛指用复数）
> - ❌ `have contributed` → <font color="#7ee787">`has contributed`</font>（主语是 `use` 单数，谓语用 has）
> - ❌ `the a slump` → 冠词重复，<font color="#ff7b72">`the` 和 `a` 不能同时修饰一个名词</font>
> - ❌ `the reading number` → <font color="#7ee787">`the number of people reading books`</font>（"读书人数"要写全）
> - ⚠️ `that it's possible to improve this situation` → <font color="#7ee787">`that suggest that improvements are possible`</font>（`bright spots` 后接 `that suggest...` 定语从句更顺）

> [!hint] 为什么
> 主语 `The widespread use`（核心词是 `use`，单数）决定谓语用 <font color="#79c0ff">`has`</font>，别被后面的 `of electronic products` 干扰。`the number of...`（……的数量）作主语时谓语也用单数。冠词 `the`/`a` 二选一，绝不能叠用。

### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：Bad management caused problems. Some improvements are possible.
>
> 改写：用 `... have contributed to the problems. But there are some bright spots...` 结构。

**你的答案**

```text
Bad management have contivuted to the problem. But there are some bright spots indicate the chance to improve.
```

**正确写法**

```text
Poor management and some poor choices have contributed to the problems. But there are some bright spots that suggest that improvements are possible.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `Bad management have` → <font color="#7ee787">`Poor management has`</font>（`management` 不可数名词，谓语用 has；`bad` → `poor` 更正式）
> - ❌ `contivuted` 拼写错误 → <font color="#7ee787">`contributed`</font>
> - ❌ `bright spots indicate` → <font color="#7ee787">`bright spots that suggest`</font>（缺关系代词 `that`，否则一个句子出现两个谓语 `are` 和 `indicate`）
> - ⚠️ `the chance to improve` → <font color="#7ee787">`improvements are possible`</font>（更贴合模板）

> [!hint] 为什么
> `there are some bright spots indicate...` 是典型错误：`there are` 已经占了谓语位置，`indicate` 就成了多余谓语。要把它变成 <font color="#79c0ff">定语从句</font> `bright spots that suggest...`，让 `that` 在从句里作主语。`contribute to`（导致/促成）的拼写要记牢。

## 让步+总起：Precisely how and why...


**句型模板**

```text
Precisely how and why ... remains an enigma, but ... sheds some light on the matter.
```

### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 父母的陪伴究竟如何以及为什么对孩子有如此关键的影响，仍是一个谜，但以下几点为此提供了一些线索。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

parents' company, crucial effect, children, remains an enigma, shed some light on the matter

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
Precisely how and why parents' company infects children so crucial remains an enigma, but flowing points shed some light on the matter.
```

**正确写法**

```text
Precisely how and why parents' company has such a crucial effect on children remains an enigma, but the following shed some light on the matter.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `infects children` → <font color="#7ee787">`has a crucial effect on children`</font>（`infect` 是"感染/传染（疾病）"，不是"影响"；"影响" = `affect` / `have an effect on`）
> - ❌ `so crucial` 位置错 → `crucial` 是形容词，不能直接修饰动词，要改成 <font color="#7ee787">`such a crucial effect`</font>
> - ❌ `flowing points` → <font color="#7ee787">`the following`</font>（"以下几点" = `the following`；`flowing` 是"流动的"）

> [!hint] 为什么
> <font color="#79c0ff">`infect`</font>（感染、传染，如病毒感染）vs <font color="#79c0ff">`affect`</font>（影响）——一字之差，意思天差地别。"对……有关键影响"的标准表达是 <font color="#7ee787">`have a crucial effect on...`</font>。`the following`（以下内容）是固定用法，`flowing`（流动的）完全是另一个词。

## 措施：...doing it right should trump...


**句型模板**

```text
As ..., doing it right should trump doing it fast.
```

---
### 练习 1：中译英

> [!tldr] 题目
> 随着国家扩大养鱼业，做对比做快更重要。

<details>
<summary>参考主题词</summary>

expand fish farming, doing it right, trump, doing it fast

</details>

**你的答案**

```text
As nation extends the fishery, doing it right should trump doing it fast. 
```

**正确写法**

```text
As the nation expands fish farming, doing it right should trump doing it fast.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `As nation` → <font color="#7ee787">`As the nation`</font>（`nation` 前要有冠词 `the`）
> - ❌ `extends the fishery` → <font color="#7ee787">`expands fish farming`</font>（"扩大规模"用 `expand`；`extend` 是"延伸/延长"。"养鱼业" = `fish farming`，`fishery` 偏"渔业/渔场"）
> - ✅ 后半句 `doing it right should trump doing it fast` 完全正确

> [!hint] 为什么
> <font color="#79c0ff">`expand`</font>（扩大规模、范围）vs <font color="#79c0ff">`extend`</font>（延伸长度、时间）：扩大产业用 `expand`。`fish farming`（水产养殖/养鱼业）是固定说法，`fishery` 更多指"渔业整体/渔场"。`trump`（动词）= 胜过、压倒，很地道的词。

### 练习 2：改写

> [!tldr] 题目
> 普通句：We are building infrastructure to improve people's health. We should do it well.
>
> 改写：用 `As we are doing this, doing it right should trump doing it fast.` 结构。

**你的答案**

```text
As we are building infrastructure to improve people's health,doing it right should trump doing it right.
```

**正确写法**

```text
As we are building more infrastructure to improve people's health, doing it right should trump doing it fast.
```

> [!danger] 批改反馈
> - ❌ `doing it right should trump doing it right` → <font color="#7ee787">`doing it right should trump doing it fast`</font>（笔误：第二个 `right` 应为 `fast`，否则变成"做对胜过做对"，意思全没了）
> - ⚠️ `building infrastructure` → <font color="#7ee787">`building more infrastructure`</font>（加 `more` 更自然）
> - ⚠️ 逗号后缺空格 → <font color="#7ee787">`health, doing`</font>

> [!hint] 为什么
> 这个句型的核心对比就是 <font color="#79c0ff">`doing it right`</font>（做对/做好）vs <font color="#79c0ff">`doing it fast`</font>（做快），`trump`（胜过）连接两者。你前半句结构套得很准，只是结尾手滑写重了，写完记得通读一遍检查这种笔误。

foreigners, traditional culture, the fact that, speaks to the importance, cultural communication

</details>

```text
The fact that more and more foreigner are more intrested in traditional culture speaks to the importance of culture communication(好像有点low了).
```

**正确写法**

```text
The fact that nowadays more and more foreigners are intrigued by our traditional culture speaks to the importance of cultural communication.
```

## 本周高频错误复盘

> [!danger] 这五类错误本周反复出现，下周重点盯
> 
> $$\begin{align} \\
> 
> &\textbf{1. with 复合结构加谓语}\text{：} \\ 
> &\quad \color{#ff7b72}{\text{With so many parents are...}}\to \color{#7ee787}{\text{With so many parents knowing...}} \\ \\
> &\textbf{2. 主谓一致}\text{：主语单复数决定谓语，别被 of 短语干扰} \\ 
> &\quad \color{#ff7b72}{\text{technology are / management have / use...have}} \\ \\
> &\textbf{3. 词性误用}\text{：名词不能直接修饰名词，形容词才能} \\ 
> &\quad \color{#ff7b72}{\text{culture communication}}\to\color{#7ee787}{\text{cultural communication}} \\ \\
> &\textbf{4. 易混词}\text{：search/research、technique/technology、infect/affect、profit/benefit、extend/expand} \\ \\
> &\textbf{5. 拼写}\text{：media、useful、pressure、cities、devices、remains、interested、contributed}
> \end{align}$$

> [!hint] 自查口诀
> 写完一句，按这个顺序过一遍：<font color="#79c0ff">谓语单复数对不对 → 有没有多余谓语（with/there be 后）→ 修饰语词性对不对 → 名词单复数和所有格 → 拼写</font>。

## 使用建议


- **先写后看**：自己写完一句，再翻回主笔记对照差距。

- **不要背答案**：重点是把<font color="#79c0ff">句型结构</font>套进不同主题。

- **主动换主题**：同一个句型，用考研真题的另外一幅图再练一遍。

- **标记错误**：写完后自查主谓一致、成分是否完整、that/what/how 是否用对。
