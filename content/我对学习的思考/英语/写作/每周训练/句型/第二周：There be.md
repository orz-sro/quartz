# 第二周：There be 句型

> 来源：田瀚博 2024 考研作文课程第二周
> 核心理念：**There be 表"客观存在"，不是"拥有"。**

---

## There be 句型基础

### There be vs have

| 句型 | 语义 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| **<font color="#79c0ff">There be</font>** | 客观存在（有） | There is a school with many teachers. |
| **<font color="#79c0ff">have</font>** | 拥有关系（财产/所属） | I have a car. |

> [!danger] 常见错误
> 学校不"拥有"教师，教师只是在学校工作。
> - ❌ This school **<font color="#ff7b72">has</font>** many teachers.
> - ✅ **<font color="#7ee787">There is</font>** a school with many teachers.

### There be 是完整句子

`There be + 名词` 本身就是完整句子，不能用逗号直接拼另一句。

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| *There is a book**<font color="#ff7b72">,</font>** I like it.* | There is a book, **<font color="#7ee787">but</font>** I like it. |
| *There is a book**<font color="#ff7b72">, however,</font>** I like it.* | There is a book, **<font color="#7ee787">but</font>** I like it. |

> [!danger] however 不能连接两句话
> `however` 是副词不是连词，不能像 `but` 那样连接两个分句。要用 however 必须拆成两句或加分号：
> - There is a book**<font color="#7ee787">;</font>** however, I like it.

### There be 后接定语的六种形式

`There be + 定语 + 名词 + 定语`，定语可前置可后置。

| 定语类型 | 示例 |
|----------|------|
| <font color="#79c0ff">形容词</font> | There is a book interesting in some aspects. |
| <font color="#79c0ff">定语从句</font> | There is a book **<font color="#7ee787">that is useful</font>**. |
| <font color="#79c0ff">介词短语</font> | There is a book **<font color="#7ee787">in my school</font>**. |
| <font color="#79c0ff">非谓语动词</font> | There is a book **<font color="#7ee787">making me happy</font>**. |
| <font color="#79c0ff">同位语</font> | There is a book, **<font color="#7ee787">a tool that is useful</font>**. |
| <font color="#79c0ff">动词不定式</font> | There is an effort **<font color="#7ee787">to make this happen</font>**. |

> [!hint] 同位语从句 vs 定语从句
> - **<font color="#7ee787">同位语从句</font>**：解释名词内容，`that` 不充当成分。如 *There is an idea **that I can play soccer**.*（idea 的内容就是踢球）
> - **<font color="#7ee787">定语从句</font>**：修饰名词特征，`that` 充当主语/宾语。如 *There is a book **that is useful**.*（that 作从句主语）

### There be 的变形

**时态变化**：

- There **<font color="#79c0ff">was</font>** a book...（过去）
- There **<font color="#79c0ff">will be</font>** a book...（将来）
- There **<font color="#79c0ff">is going to be</font>** a better book.

**系动词替换**（be 可换其他系动词）：

- There **<font color="#7ee787">becomes</font>** a better book.
- There **<font color="#7ee787">remains</font>** a problem.
- There **<font color="#7ee787">seems to be</font>**...

**here be**：与 there be 语义相同，仅方位不同。

## 外刊神句

### 总览

| 序号 | 功能 | 句型 |
|------|------|------|
| 1 | 补充论据 | There is plenty of evidence... |
| 2 | 提出论点 | ...but the real prize is... |
| 3 | 背景句 | ...are bound to arise |
| 4 | 总起句 | For anyone who may doubt... |
| 5 | 背景+观点 | Among the facts is that... |
| 6 | 过渡句 | ...in its early stages... |
| 7 | 观点句 | The very fact comes at... |
| 8 | 背景+总起 | ...bright spots... |
| 9 | 让步+总起 | Precisely how and why... |
| 10 | 措施 | ...doing it right should trump... |
| 11 | 观点句 | The fact that... speaks to... |
| 12 | 让步+观点 | Although ... may suggest otherwise |

### 1. 补充论据：There is plenty of evidence...

**结构**：

```text
There is plenty of evidence showing/illustrating/demonstrating that + 完整句子
```

> [!example] 外刊原句
> There is plenty of evidence showing/illustrating **<font color="#79c0ff">that</font>** losing weight isn't easy and **<font color="#79c0ff">that</font>** it is rarely sustained in the long term.

> [!example] 考研例句
> There is plenty of evidence showing **<font color="#79c0ff">that</font>** the health level of citizens would be greatly improved if the government could provide them enough infrastructure for exercising.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> - There is plenty of evidence illustrating **<font color="#7ee787">that AI is becoming more and more advanced</font>**.
> - There is plenty of evidence illustrating **<font color="#7ee787">that parents should be a role model for their children</font>**.

### 2. 提出论点：...but the real prize is...

**结构**：

```text
Sth is certainly useful, but the real prize is that + 完整句子
```

- `the real prize is` = 真正的收获/价值在于

> [!example] 外刊原句
> Better methods for predicting rogue waves are certainly useful: some can sink even the biggest ships. But **<font color="#79c0ff">the real prize is</font>** the visibility that Dr Hafner's approach offers into what the neural network was doing.

> [!example] 考研例句
> Strong willpower is certainly useful in study, but **<font color="#79c0ff">the real prize is</font>** that it will help us go through one obstacle after another, as there is no specific destination in our whole life.

> [!hint] 改写思路
> 原句：Computers are useful in many aspects.
> 改写：Computers are certainly useful in many aspects, but **<font color="#7ee787">the real prize is that</font>** AI is becoming more and more popular.

### 3. 背景句：...are bound to arise

**结构**：

```text
With so many ..., many problems are bound to arise.
```

- `be bound to` = 必然发生（= must happen / will definitely occur）

> [!example] 外刊原句
> With so many people crammed into a closed mountain valley, many environmental and social problems **<font color="#79c0ff">are bound to arise</font>**.

> [!example] 考研例句
> With so many parents knowing nothing about their child's pressure in study, many physical and mental problems **<font color="#79c0ff">are bound to arise</font>**.

### 4. 总起句：For anyone who may doubt...

**结构**：

```text
For anyone who may doubt ..., ... provides strong evidence that ...
```

> [!example] 外刊原句
> In the meantime, **<font color="#79c0ff">for anyone who may doubt</font>** humanity's ability to affect the weather, Dr Thornton's work **<font color="#79c0ff">provides strong evidence that</font>** it can.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **<font color="#79c0ff">For anyone who may doubt</font>** the advantages of listening to lectures about other majors, the following reasons would **<font color="#79c0ff">provide strong evidence</font>**.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> For anyone who doubt my love to you, my heart **<font color="#7ee787">provides strong evidence that</font>** I am deeply in love with you.

### 5. 背景+观点：Among the facts is that...

**结构**：

```text
Among the facts that have emerged about ... is that + 完整句子
```

- 倒装结构：正常语序 *That ... is among the facts.*

> [!example] 外刊原句
> Among the facts that have emerged about it **<font color="#79c0ff">is that</font>** individual birds do, indeed, have distinct personalities.

> [!example] 考研例句
> Environment protection is a well-studied subject, and **<font color="#79c0ff">among the facts that have emerged about it is that</font>** there are still a host of people who have no idea about the urgency of the situation.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> Among many facts about me **<font color="#7ee787">is that</font>** I love you.（在很多关于我的事实之中，我爱你是其中一个）

### 6. 过渡句：...in its early stages...

**结构**：

```text
A is in its early stages, but is already full of B.
```

> [!example] 外刊原句
> The race to dominate the field of self-driving cars **<font color="#79c0ff">is in its early stages</font>**, but **<font color="#79c0ff">is already full of intrigue</font>**.

> [!example] 考研例句
> The race to find an appropriate job **<font color="#79c0ff">is in its early stages</font>**, but **<font color="#79c0ff">is already full of obstacles</font>** for the young.

### 7. 观点句：The very fact comes at...

**结构**：

```text
The very fact of A comes at a bad/good time when ...
```

- `the very fact` 中的 `very` 是形容词，"恰恰、正是"的意思，表强调

> [!example] 外刊原句
> But **<font color="#79c0ff">the very fact of the lawsuit comes at a bad time</font>** for Uber, which is under fire for having a rough-and-tumble culture that values winning at all costs.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **<font color="#79c0ff">The very fact that</font>** students would take great advantages from AI **<font color="#79c0ff">comes at a good time when</font>** the advanced technologies nowadays could provide convenient access for them to acquire all sorts of knowledge.

### 8. 背景+总起：...bright spots...

**结构**：

```text
A and B have contributed to the problems. But there are some bright spots ...
```

- `bright spots` = 亮点、积极面

> [!example] 外刊原句
> Poor management ... and some poor choices by many fishers **<font color="#79c0ff">have contributed to the problems</font>**. But **<font color="#79c0ff">there are some bright spots</font>**, small and scattered, that suggest that improvements are possible.

> [!example] 考研例句
> The wide range use of electronic products **<font color="#79c0ff">has contributed to</font>** the plummeting number of people reading books. But **<font color="#79c0ff">there are some bright spots</font>** that suggest that improvements are possible.

### 9. 让步+总起：Precisely how and why...

**结构**：

```text
Precisely how and why ... remains an enigma, but ... sheds some light on the matter.
```

- `remains an enigma` = 仍是个谜
- `sheds some light on the matter` = 为此事提供了一些线索/启示

> [!example] 外刊原句
> **<font color="#79c0ff">Precisely how and why</font>** meddling with day and night cycles has such a dire effect on the liver **<font color="#79c0ff">remains an enigma</font>**, but a study just published in Cancer Cell ... **<font color="#79c0ff">sheds some light on the matter</font>**.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **<font color="#79c0ff">Precisely how and why</font>** parents' company has such a crucial effect on children **<font color="#79c0ff">remains an enigma</font>**, but the following **<font color="#79c0ff">shed some light on the matter</font>**. The first effect is that ...

### 10. 措施：...doing it right should trump...

**结构**：

```text
As ..., doing it right should trump doing it fast.
```

- `trump`（动词）= 胜过、超越

> [!example] 外刊原句
> As the nation expands fish farming, **<font color="#79c0ff">doing it right should trump doing it fast</font>**.

> [!example] 考研例句
> As advanced technologies are making great changes to people's lives, **<font color="#79c0ff">doing it right should trump doing it fast</font>**.

> [!hint] 改写思路
> 原句：We should build more infrastructure about improving people's health.
> 改写：As we are doing this, **<font color="#7ee787">doing it right should trump doing it fast</font>**.

### 11. 观点句：The fact that... speaks to...

**结构**：

```text
The fact that + 句子 + speaks to the importance of ...
```

- `speak to` = 说明、表明（不是"对…说话"）

> [!example] 外刊原句
> **<font color="#79c0ff">The fact that</font>** we have had this debate before **<font color="#79c0ff">speaks its importance</font>**.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **<font color="#79c0ff">The fact that</font>** nowadays more and more foreigners are intrigued to our traditional culture **<font color="#79c0ff">speaks to the importance of</font>** cultural communication.

### 12. 让步+观点：Although ... may suggest otherwise

**结构**：

```text
Although some recent experiences may suggest otherwise, + 完整句子
```

- `otherwise` = 另一面、相反的情况（这里指"相反的迹象"）

> [!example] 外刊原句
> **<font color="#79c0ff">Although some recent experiences may suggest otherwise</font>**, fishers and scientists need each other in order to succeed.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **<font color="#79c0ff">Although some recent experiences may suggest otherwise</font>**, universities should provide enough support to improve students' physical conditions.
