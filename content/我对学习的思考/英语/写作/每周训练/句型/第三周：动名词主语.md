# 第三周：动名词主语

> 来源：田瀚博 2025 考研作文课程第三周
> 核心理念：**用动名词（doing）把动作变成句子的主角。**

---

## 动名词主语的基础

### 写作逻辑：让动作当主角

英文句子的主语必须是名词。当你想表达一个"动作"作为句子的核心时，就要把动词加上 `-ing` 变成**动名词**，让它既有**动词的意思**又有**名词的身份**。

| 中文思维 | 英文（动名词主语） | 说明 |
|---------|------------------|------|
| 考研是好的 | **Taking the postgraduate exam** is good for us. | "考研"这个动作变主语 |
| 参加研究生入学考试是必要的 | **Joining the exam preparation** is necessary. | 整个动作短语作主语 |

> [!hint] 一句话理解
> 动名词主语就是把中文里一个动词性的想法变成"做……是……"的句式。**关键是中文思维先行**——先想一想你要表达的动作是什么，再用 `doing` 把它变主语。
>
> 复习累了休息五分钟，别刷手机，把你脑子里正在想的事情用动名词开头写一句话。坚持就是这个道理。

### 谓语用第三人称单数（铁律）

动名词短语作主语时，整个短语视为**单数**，谓语动词用 `does / is / has`。

```text
Playing basketball makes me happy.                  ✅
Reading books broadens our horizons.                ✅
Providing evidence is what really matters.          ✅
Providing evidence are what really matters.         ❌
```

> [!danger] 这是最高频错误
> 动名词主语后谓语忘加 `s` / `is`。这周所有的练习，写完后只检查一件事：**主语是 `Doing...` → 谓语是不是单数**。

### 动名词短语作为整体

动名词可以带自己的宾语，形成动名词短语整体作主语：

```text
Reading books is good for your mind.
```

`Reading`（动名词）+ `books`（它的宾语）= 整个短语作主语，不是"读书"拆开各算各的。

> [!danger] 常见错误：动名词与谓语"打架"
> 写完动名词主语后必须检查：这个动名词代表的**动作能否合理发出后面的谓语**。
>
> - ❌ Becoming a good player is evidence.（"成为"这个动作怎么能是"证据"？）
> - ❌ Providing evidence has been provided...（动名词"提供证据"已经是主动了，不能再"被提供"）
> - ✅ Providing evidence is what he does.（正确：提供证据是他做的事）

### 状语从句的位置

```text
Playing basketball makes me happy when I am in China.
```

`when I am in China` 修饰整个主句 → "在中国时，打篮球让我高兴"。

```text
Playing basketball when I am in China makes me happy.
```

`when I am in China` 修饰 `playing basketball` → "只有在中国打篮球时才让我高兴"。

> [!hint] 怎么选位置
> 写作时安全做法：状语从句放在句末，用逗号隔开。从句修饰的是 **makes me happy** 这个谓语整体。

## 外刊神句

### 总览

| 序号 | 功能 | 句型 |
|------|------|------|
| 1 | 批判观点 | Relying on... is not an ideal approach for... |
| 2 | 功能替代 | A does the job of B |
| 3 | 多层因果 | A was caused by B as a result of C |
| 4 | 主导地位 | A has moved B into the driving seat of C |
| 5 | 对立立场 | A is the opposite of everything that... |
| 6 | 论据支撑 | Many books and articles have been written..., and often become bestsellers... |
| 7 | 观点分化 | These views illustrate the extreme polarity... |
| 8 | 永恒话题 | Efforts to do X are a perennial topic... |
| 9 | 大量论据 | There is a wealth of evidence that... |
| 10 | 关联揭示 | The association between A and B is obvious... |
| 11 | 重要作用 | Doing A plays a vital role in doing B |
| 12 | 功能使然 | Doing A enables B to C |

### 1. 批判观点：Relying on... is not an ideal approach for...

**结构**：

```text
Relying on + 名词/动名词 + is not an ideal approach for + 名词/doing
```

表达"依赖 A 不是实现 B 的好方法"。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> **Relying on** the child itself, which is quite vulnerable to the virtual world, **is not an ideal approach for** acquiring knowledge without the company of parents.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **Relying on** using AI **is not an ideal approach for** learning in class.

> [!hint] approach to 后面的 to 是介词
> `approach to doing` 中的 `to` 是**介词**，后面用 `doing`，不能用动词原形。同理：`the key to doing`、`a solution to doing`。

> [!hint] 举一反三
> - **Relying on** memorization **is not an ideal approach for** mastering a subject.
> - **Relying on** social media alone **is not an ideal approach for** building genuine relationships.

### 2. 功能替代：A does the job of B

**结构**：

```text
A does the job (of B)
```

`do the job` = 起作用/完成任务。`job` 回指前文提到的某个目标。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> ...and this machine can **do the job**.

> [!example] 考研例句
> As for students who are wondering how to improve learning efficiency, using AI may **do the job**.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> - For those eager to expand their knowledge, reading classics can **do the job**.
> - When it comes to staying connected, social media can **do the job**.

### 3. 多层因果：A was caused by B as a result of C

**结构**：

```text
A was caused by B as a result of C
```

链式因果：C → B → A。用介词短语 `as a result of` 代替动词，增加句式多样性。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> Poor performance in school was **caused by** irrational parental behavior **as a result of** unhealthy habits.

> [!example] 考研例句
> Game addiction among teenagers can be **caused by** a lack of parental supervision **as a result of** parents' busy work schedules.

> [!hint] 改写思路
> 不用这个结构：Because of C, B causes A.
> 用这个结构：A is caused by B **as a result of** C.
> 后者更紧凑，层次更深，更适合考研论述段落。

### 4. 主导地位：A has moved B into the driving seat of C

**结构**：

```text
A has moved B into the driving seat of C
```

`driving seat` = 驾驶座 → **主导地位**。A 使 B 在 C 领域占据主导。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> The rapid adoption of synthetic biology **has moved** the biotech industry **into the driving seat of** industrial production.

> [!example] 考研例句
> The development of technology **has moved** AI **into the driving seat of** modern education.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> - Social media **has moved** online influencers **into the driving seat of** youth culture.
> - Online learning **has moved** self-study **into the driving seat of** skill acquisition.

### 5. 对立立场：A is the opposite of everything that... stands for

**结构**：

```text
A is the opposite of everything that + 名词 + stands for
```

强烈反对：A 是 X 所代表的一切的对立面。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> This attitude **is the opposite of everything that** modern science **stands for**.

> [!example] 考研例句
> Laughing at others' enthusiasm **is the opposite of everything that** modern education **stands for**.

> [!hint] 变体使用
> - A **is the opposite of** everything that modern society stands for.（反对立场）
> - A **is on the same side with** everything that XX stands for.（赞同立场）
> - `modern society` 可换成 `education`、`traditional culture`、`academic integrity` 等。

### 6. 论据支撑：Many books... have been written..., and often become bestsellers...

**结构**：

```text
Many books and articles have been written to illustrate X, and often become bestsellers, indicating that...
```

利用"畅销书现象"间接证明观点普遍性。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> Many books and articles **have been written to illustrate** the threads about X, and **often become bestsellers, indicating that** this idea is true to many.

> [!example] 考研例句
> Many books and articles **have been written to illustrate** the dangers of smartphone addiction, and **often become bestsellers, indicating that** this concern is shared by many.

> [!caution] 别当万能模板
> 这句话非常长，使用时务必把 X 换成具体话题。不要上考场先不看题就写这句。

### 7. 观点分化：These views illustrate the extreme polarity...

**结构**：

```text
These views illustrate the extreme polarity in the positions people take when discussing X
```

`polarity` = 极端分化（polar → 两极）。描述争议性话题中的观点对立。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> **These views illustrate the extreme polarity in the positions people take when discussing** the topic.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **These views in the picture illustrate the extreme polarity in the positions people take when discussing** whether to choose easy courses or challenging ones.

> [!hint] 使用场景
> 适合英语一图画作文中有争议的题目。放在第一段末尾或第二段开头，接着引出两方面观点：
> ```text
> Some believe that..., while others argue that...
> ```

### 8. 永恒话题：Efforts to do X are a perennial topic...

**结构**：

```text
Efforts to do X are a perennial topic in discussions of how to Y
```

`perennial` = 常年不断的（松树常绿 → perennial）。引出长期议题。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> **Efforts to** encourage innovation **are a perennial topic in discussions of how to** face challenges.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **Efforts to** pick various courses in college **are a perennial topic in discussions of how to** take advantage of college time.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> - **Efforts to** balance growth and environmental protection **are a perennial topic in discussions of how to** achieve sustainable development.
> - **Efforts to** improve education **are a perennial topic in discussions of how to** cultivate well-rounded talents.

### 9. 大量论据：There is a wealth of evidence that...

**结构**：

```text
There is a wealth of evidence that + 完整句子
```

`a wealth of` = 丰富的、大量的（替代 `a lot of` / `many`）。

> [!example] 考研例句
> **There is a wealth of evidence that** regular exercise contributes to both physical and mental health.

> [!hint] 其他量词积累
> - a **wealth of** information / evidence（丰富的）
> - a **multitude of** factors / challenges（众多的）
> - a **variety of** approaches / methods（多样的）

### 10. 关联揭示：The association between A and B is obvious...

**结构**：

```text
The association between A and B is obvious, which indicates a key area for C
```

先指出关联明显，再说明揭示的关键领域。

> [!example] 外刊原句
> **The association between** poverty and health problems **is obvious, which indicates a key area for** intervention.

> [!example] 考研例句
> **The association between** the use of AI and learning efficiency **is obvious, which indicates a key area for** improvement.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> - **The association between** a sedentary lifestyle and health issues **is obvious, which indicates a key area for** intervention.
> - **The association between** reading and academic success **is obvious, which indicates a key area for** policy improvement.

### 11. 重要作用：Doing A plays a vital role in doing B

**结构**：

```text
Doing A plays a vital role in doing B
```

最简单、最安全、最万能的动名词主语句式。动名词主语 + `play a vital role in` + 动名词宾语。

> [!example] 考研例句
> **Cultivating good study habits plays a vital role in** achieving academic success.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> - **Raising public awareness plays a vital role in** protecting the environment.
> - **Promoting cultural exchange plays a vital role in** enhancing mutual understanding.
> - **Embracing technological innovation plays a vital role in** improving productivity.

### 12. 功能使然：Doing A enables B to C

**结构**：

```text
Doing A enables B to C
```

把 `By doing A, B can C` 升级为动名词主语结构，更书面化。

> [!example] 考研例句
> **Reading extensively enables students to** broaden their horizons and think critically.

> [!hint] 举一反三
> - **Using AI tools enables workers to** focus on more creative tasks.
> - **Exercising regularly enables people to** maintain physical and mental well-being.
> - **Mastering a foreign language enables young people to** gain more opportunities.

- **核心目标**：在考场上写出正确的动名词主语结构，而不是回避它。
